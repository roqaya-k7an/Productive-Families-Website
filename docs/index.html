Empowering Productive Families to Sell Online

An e-commerce platform that connects low-income productive families directly with customers — helping them market handmade food, crafts, and products, and transform from dependent families into productive ones that contribute to economic development.

Explore the Project View Source Code
About the Project

A marketplace built for the community

Productive families rely on the handicrafts they produce — food, health & beauty products, accessories, handicrafts and clothes. Marketing them through social media is slow and costly. This website gives them one simple, fast place to reach customers and grow their income.

🎯

Easy Marketing

Give productive families a simple, effective way to market their products to a large segment of society.

🤝

Connect People

Create an environment that brings productive families and clients together in one place.

⏱️

Save Time & Effort

Replace scattered ads and phone calls with a single dedicated online storefront.

📈

Grow Income

Reach the largest possible number of shoppers and support economic development.

Key Features

Four roles, one platform

Each user type gets a tailored dashboard and permissions, while visitors can browse the storefront freely.

🛠️

Admin

  • View & manage all users
  • Manage products & families
  • Oversee clients & agents
  • Follow up on all operations
👩‍🍳

Productive Family

  • Register & log in
  • Add, edit & delete products
  • View incoming orders
  • Accept or refuse demands
🛍️

Client

  • Browse by category
  • Add items to cart
  • Send product demands
  • Track order status
🚚

Delivery Agent

  • View delivery orders
  • Accept or cancel deliveries
  • Manage their account
  • Track active requests

Browse five product categories — no login required:

🍽️ Food
💄 Health & Beauty
👜 Accessories
🧶 Handicrafts
👗 Clothes
